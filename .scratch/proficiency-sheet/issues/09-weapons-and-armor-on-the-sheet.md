# 09: Weapons and armor on the Proficiency Sheet

**What to build:** Weapon and armor proficiencies come from the sheet, from class base proficiencies, subclass features, feats and race, keeping the Categorical Weapon Proficiencies grouping (a category supersedes its individual weapons). A characterization test first records existing characters' armor and weapon proficiencies and proves them unchanged except where a grant is newly applied (for example the Life Domain's heavy armor).
