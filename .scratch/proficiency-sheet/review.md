  - You don't need to share a language with the creature for it to understand your telepathic utterances, but the creature must be able to understand at least one language

## Warlock/The Hexblade

- Hex Warrior (level 1)
  - You gain proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons
  - Whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch one weapon that you are proficient with and that lacks the two-handed property
- Hexblade's Curse (level 1)
  - The bonus equals your proficiency bonus

## Warlock/The Fathomless

- Tentacle of the Deeps (level 1)
  - You can summon the tentacle a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest

## Warlock/The Genie

- Bottled Respite (level 1)
  - You can remain inside the vessel up to a number of hours equal to twice your proficiency bonus
- Genie's Wrath (level 1)
  - Once during each of your turns when you hit with an attack roll, you can deal extra damage to the target equal to your proficiency bonus
- Genie's Vessel (level 1)
  - Its hit points equal your warlock level plus your proficiency bonus, and it is immune to poison and psychic damage
- Elemental Gift (level 6)
  - You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Sanctuary Vessel (level 10)
  - In addition, anyone (including you) who remains within the vessel for at least 10 minutes gains the benefit of finishing a short rest, and anyone can add your proficiency bonus to the number of hit points they regain if they spend any Hit Dice as part of a short rest there

## Warlock/The Undead

- Form of Dread (level 1)
  - You can transform a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest

## Wizard

- Spellcasting (level 1)
  - Spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your INT modifier
  - Spell attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your INT modifier

## Wizard/School of Abjuration

- Improved Abjuration (level 10)
  - Beginning at 10th level, when you cast an abjuration spell that requires you to make an ability check as a part of casting that spell (as in counterspell and dispel magic), you add your proficiency bonus to that ability check

## Wizard/School of Divination

- The Third Eye (level 10)
  - You can read any language

## Wizard/School of Necromancy

- Undead Thralls (level 6)
  - The creature adds your proficiency bonus to its weapon damage rolls

## Wizard/School of Transmutation

- Transmuter's Stone (level 6)
  - Proficiency in Constitution saving throws

## Wizard/Bladesinging

- Bladesong (level 2)
  - You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses of it when you finish a long rest
- Training in War and Song (Bladesinging) (level 2)
  - When you adopt this tradition at 2nd level, you gain proficiency with light armor, and you gain proficiency with one type of one-handed melee weapon of your choice
  - You also gain proficiency in the Performance skill if you don't already have it

## Wizard/Order of Scribes

- Manifest Mind (level 6)
  - You can do so a number of times per day equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest

## Feat

- Adept of the Red Robes (level 0)
  - You can balance fate in this way a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Agent of Order (level 0)
  - Stasis Strike Once per turn, when you damage a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself, you can deal an extra 1d8 force damage to the target, and it must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + the modifier of the spellcasting ability you chose for the Scion of the Outer Planes feat) as spectral bindings try to ensnare it
  - You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Artificer Initiate (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice, and you can use that type of tool as a spellcasting focus for any spell you cast that uses Intelligence as its spellcasting ability
- Baleful Scion (level 0)
  - The necrotic damage equals 1d6 + your proficiency bonus, and you regain a number of hit points equal to this necrotic damage dealt
  - You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Chef (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency with cook's utensils if you don't already have it
  - You can prepare enough of this food for a number of creatures equal to 4 + your proficiency bonus
  - With one hour of work or when you finish a long rest, you can cook a number of treats equal to your proficiency bonus
  - A creature can use a bonus action to eat one of those treats to gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus
- Crossbow Expert (level 0)
  - You ignore the loading quality of crossbows with which you are proficient
- Defensive Duelist (level 0)
  - When you are wielding a finesse weapon with which you are proficient and another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to your AC for that attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you
- Dragon Fear (level 0)
  - Instead of exhaling destructive energy, you can expend a use of your Breath Weapon trait to roar, forcing each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier)
- Ember of the Fire Giant (level 0)
  - Each creature of your choice in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the modifier of the ability increased by this feat)
  - On a failed save, a creature takes fire damage equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus, and it has the blinded condition until the start of your next turn
  - You can use your Searing Ignition a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus (but no more than once per turn), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Fury of the Frost Giant (level 0)
  - The creature must make a Constitution saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the modifier of the ability increased by this feat)
  - On a failed save, the creature takes cold damage equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus, and its speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn
  - You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Gift of the Chromatic Dragon (level 0)
  - You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Gift of the Gem Dragon (level 0)
  - The creature that dealt damage to you must make a Strength saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat)
  - You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Gift of the Metallic Dragon (level 0)
  - You grant a bonus to the target's AC equal to your proficiency bonus against that attack roll, potentially causing it to miss
  - You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Great Weapon Master (level 0)
  - Before you make a melee attack with a heavy weapon that you are proficient with, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll
- Guile of the Cloud Giant (level 0)
  - You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Gunner (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency with firearms (see "DMG" in the Dungeon Master's Guide)
- Initiate of High Sorcery (level 0)
  - | Solinari | Choose two from comprehend languages, detect evil and good, protection from evil and good, and shield |
- Keenness of the Stone Giant (level 0)
  - On a hit, the rock deals 1d10 force damage, and the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the spellcasting ability modifier) or have the prone condition
  - You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Knight of the Crown (level 0)
  - You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Knight of the Rose (level 0)
  - If the target can see or hear you, the target gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the ability score increased by this feat
  - You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Knight of the Sword (level 0)
  - The target must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat)
  - You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Linguist (level 0)
  - You have studied languages and codes, gaining the following benefits:
  - You learn three languages of your choice
  - Others can't decipher a code you create unless you teach them, they succeed on an Intelligence check (DC equal to your Intelligence score + your proficiency bonus), or they use magic to decipher it
- Martial Adept (level 0)
  - If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a saving throw to resist the maneuver's effects, the saving throw DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice)
- Observant (level 0)
  - If you can see a creature's mouth while it is speaking a language you understand, you can interpret what it's saying by reading its lips
- Poisoner (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency with the poisoner's kit if you don't already have it
  - With one hour of work using a poisoner's kit and expending 50 gp worth of materials, you can create a number of doses of potent poison equal to your proficiency bonus
- Prodigy (level 0)
  - You gain one skill proficiency of your choice, one tool proficiency of your choice, and fluency in one language of your choice
  - Choose one skill in which you have proficiency
  - You gain expertise with that skill, which means your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make with it
  - The skill you choose must be one that isn't already benefiting from a feature, such as Expertise, that doubles your proficiency bonus
- Quicksmithing (level 0)
  - In addition, you have proficiency with artisan's tools (quicksmith's tools)
- Resilient (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency in saving throws using the chosen ability
- Righteous Heritor (level 0)
  - Soothe Pain When you or a creature within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus
  - You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Rune Shaper (level 0)
  - Comprehend Languages You learn the comprehend languages spell
  - Rune Magic You know a number of runes equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down), chosen from the Rune Spells table
- Sharpshooter (level 0)
  - Before you make an attack with a ranged weapon that you are proficient with, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll
- Skill Expert (level 0)
  - You have honed your proficiency with particular skills, granting you the following benefits:
  - You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice
  - Choose one skill in which you have proficiency
  - You gain expertise with that skill, which means your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make with it
  - The skill you choose must be one that isn't already benefiting from a feature, such as Expertise, that doubles your proficiency bonus
- Skilled (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency in any combination of three skills or tools of your choice
- Soul of the Storm Giant (level 0)
  - In addition, attack rolls against you have disadvantage, and whenever another creature starts its turn within the aura, you can force the creature to make a Strength saving throw (DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + the modifier of the ability increased by this feat)
  - You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Squire of Solamnia (level 0)
  - You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, but a use is expended only if the attack hits
- Strike of the Giants (level 0)
  - The saving throw DC for these effects equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Constitution modifier
  - You can use this feat a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest
- Tavern Brawler (level 0)
  - You are proficient with improvised weapons
- Telekinetic (level 0)
  - When you do so, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat) or be moved 5 feet toward you or away from you
- Telepathic (level 0)
  - Your telepathic utterances are in a language you know, and the creature understands you only if it knows that language
- Vigor of the Hill Giant (level 0)
  - Iron Stomach Whenever you eat food as part of a short rest and spend one or more Hit Dice to regain hit points, you regain additional hit points equal to your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus
- Weapon Master (level 0)
  - You gain proficiency with four weapons of your choice
