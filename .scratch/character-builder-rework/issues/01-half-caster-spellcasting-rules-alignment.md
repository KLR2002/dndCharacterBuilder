# 01: 2014 Half-Caster Spellcasting Rules Alignment

**What to build:** Ensure 1st-level Paladins and Rangers do not gain spellcasting profiles, spell slots, or prepared limits prematurely under 2014 rules. At level 1, a character with only Paladin or Ranger levels sees a message indicating they do not yet have spellcasting capabilities. Upon reaching level 2 in either class, the Spells tab unlocks the spellcasting profile with 1st-level spell slots, spell save DC, attack bonus, and prepared/known spell management consistent with 2014 rules.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** closed

- [x] A 1st-level Paladin or Ranger has no active spell profile and 0 spell slots.
- [x] Reaching 2nd level in Paladin or Ranger unlocks spellcasting, displays spell save DC and spell attack bonus, and grants two 1st-level spell slots.
- [x] In multiclass characters, Paladin and Ranger levels contribute to the multiclass spell slot caster level only if the class level is 2 or higher (per 2014 multiclassing rules: floor of level divided by 2).
- [x] All unit checks for spellcasting pass.
