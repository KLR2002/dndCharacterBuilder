# 02: Half-Feat Ability Score Increases in ASI/Feat Selection

**What to build:** Support ability score increases granted by feats ("half-feats") when a player selects a feat during class level-up ASI/Feat slots. When a feat offers an ability score increase (e.g. Actor, Fey Touched, Observant, Resilient), the player can choose the target ability score from the options the feat allows. The +1 bonus applies to the character's effective ability scores and propagates to ability modifiers, saving throws, skills, passive scores, DCs, and weapon attack/damage bonuses.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** closed

- [x] When selecting a feat that grants an ability score increase, the UI presents options for the eligible ability score(s).
- [x] The chosen ability increase is saved with the feat selection on that class level slot.
- [x] Effective ability scores include +1 from selected half-feats.
- [x] Removing or changing the feat removes or reassigns the corresponding ability increase cleanly.
- [x] Unit checks verify score calculation and feat selection transitions.
