Dice prototype throwaway

Question this answers: do we mean the same thing by (1) solid 3D dice drawn with plain 2D shapes, (2) Tumble (turn in place, stop on the drawn face) versus Throw (dice fly across the screen, the face on top is the result), and (3) the Roll Expression field (NdX, + -, numbers, a(d20,...) / d(d20,...) that take only d20s, a comma that separates independent Rolls, a count before a function that repeats the whole Roll, and one colour per Roll)? Nothing here is saved; the three script modules (expression, solids, physics) are written to be lifted into the app.

Dice Animation Accent Tumble: up to 10 solids in the result card. Throw: up to 20 solids over the whole screen, thrown from the round button at the bottom right.
