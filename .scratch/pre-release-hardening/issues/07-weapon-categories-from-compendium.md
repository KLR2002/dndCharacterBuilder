# 07: Weapon categories from the Compendium

**What to build:** A Way of the Kensei monk is offered every simple or martial weapon without the heavy or special property, whichever book it comes from. The items scraper carries each weapon's simple or martial category from the source data, the items data is regenerated by a scraper run, and the Kensei pool reads the category from the Compendium instead of the app's standard lists. Magic variants are still never taken for their base weapon.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The scraped items carry a weapon category, and a scraper test covers extraction with invented items.
- [ ] The Kensei pool reads the category from the Compendium; a weapon from another book that the data places in the pool is offered, covered by a test with an invented weapon.
- [ ] The app's standard simple and martial lists are no longer used for the Kensei rule.
- [ ] The Kensei and weapons characterization tests stay green, or change only through their recorders with a reviewed diff explained in the Landed note.
- [ ] The items data changed only through a scraper run, and the SRD Baseline through the prune script in its own commit.
- [ ] `npm run check:all` passes.
