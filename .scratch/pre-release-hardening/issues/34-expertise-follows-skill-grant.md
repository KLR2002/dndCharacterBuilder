# 34: A skill grant that doubles its bonus gives Expertise on the skill picked

**What to build:** Owner bug report, and a data rule. Blessings of Knowledge grants two skills and doubles the proficiency bonus for them. The app handles that by hand: it adds a separate "Blessings of Knowledge (Cleric Level 1)" Expertise milestone (`src/rules/expertise.ts`, `isKnowledgeCleric` in `src/rules/skills.ts`) whose picks are independent of the skills the player picked in the Blessings skill choice, and `knowledgeExpertiseSkills` (`src/rules/proficiencySheet.ts`) turns an Expertise pick on a Knowledge skill into a proficiency. So the player can pick Arcana and History as the granted skills and Nature and Religion as Expertise, and ends up proficient in four skills. The rule must follow the grant: the skills picked are the skills with Expertise. Other catalog features do the same, with fixed skills (Scout's Survivalist, Royal Envoy), so the mark is data on the grant, not code for one feature.

## Design notes

- Scraper: a `skills` Proficiency Grant (fixed or choice) gets `expertise: true` when its feature's own text says the proficiency bonus is doubled for the granted skills. Add the optional field to the grant type (`dnd5eToolsScrap/src/types/proficiency.ts`) and extract it where grants are extracted (`dnd5eToolsScrap/src/parser/proficiency-grants.ts`, with the reviewed override file in `dnd5eToolsScrap/src/overrides/` for sentences the matcher cannot parse). Follow ADR 0015: a scraper test fails when a feature's text says the bonus is doubled and the feature has neither an `expertise` grant nor an explicit "reviewed, not a skill grant" mark. Expertise features that choose among skills the character already holds (the Rogue's, the Bard's, Skill Expert, Prodigy) are the existing Expertise milestones and must not be marked: they have no skill grant of their own. Decide the matcher's wording by reading the real features in the live catalog (`.cache/live-catalog/catalog.json`; `npm run catalog:live` rebuilds it); the features to check are the subclass features named above, and the report lists every feature the matcher marked. Use no real prose in tests: invent a feature whose text doubles the bonus.
- Sheet: a skill entry that comes from an `expertise: true` grant (fixed names, or the player's pick of a choice grant) carries Expertise. `skills()` (`src/rules/skills.ts`) reads it from the sheet entry, next to `character.chosenExpertise`. Expertise from a grant is derived, never stored in `chosenExpertise`: it vanishes with the grant and the pick, like every other sheet entry (ADR 0015). Check that the skill row, the sheet and the card agree for fixed and for chosen grants, and that a skill held from two sources keeps Expertise if either grant marks it.
- Remove the hand rule: the `cleric_knowledge_1` milestone, `knowledgeExpertiseSkills`, `KNOWLEDGE_SKILLS`, and `isKnowledgeCleric` if nothing else reads it. The Blessings picker stays as the one place the two skills are chosen; show that those picks carry Expertise (see ticket 35 for where; here, the pick count and the sheet are the contract).
- Saved characters: a Knowledge Cleric saved under the old rule has Knowledge skills in `chosenExpertise` and possibly no pick in the Blessings skill choice. On load, for each expertise-marked choice grant with free pick slots, move names from `chosenExpertise` that are in the grant's pool into the grant's pick, then the existing Expertise pruning drops what is left over with its review notice. Put the move beside the other `move...` migrations in `src/rules/proficiencyPicks.ts`, generic over the grant mark, with a test over an invented class and subclass. The sheet a saved character shows must not lose a skill it showed before.
- Bump `REQUIRED_CATALOG_FORMAT` (`src/services/compendiumStorage.ts`) from 6 to 7: a catalog cached before this has no `expertise` marks, and the bump makes the app ask for a re-sync. Update the tests and fixtures that name the format. Regenerate the tracked data only by the sanctioned ways (`npm --prefix dnd5eToolsScrap run enrich:grants`, then `node scripts/prune-to-srd.mjs` in its own commit); say in the report what changed in the data files.
- Test first at the sheet seam: an invented subclass whose feature has a choice skill grant marked `expertise`: picking two skills gives Expertise on those two and proficiency on no others; a fixed marked grant gives Expertise on its skills; an unmarked grant gives none; removing the subclass removes both. `npm run check:live` must pass: add an invariant that every `expertise` grant is on a `skills` category grant.
