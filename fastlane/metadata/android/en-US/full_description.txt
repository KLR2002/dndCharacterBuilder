<p>A character builder and character sheet for <b>Dungeons &amp; Dragons 5th Edition (2014 rules)</b>. Your characters are stored only on your device.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Build a character step by step: race, class, background, ability scores, skills and starting equipment.</li>
<li>Character sheet with Builder, Stats, Actions, Spells, Features, Inventory and Journal tabs.</li>
<li>Calculates armor class, attacks, proficiencies, spell slots and level-up progression.</li>
<li>Comes with the SRD rules and works offline.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Network use</b></p>
<p>The app works without an internet connection. You can choose to sync a larger catalog in the app. The sync downloads game data from a third-party GitHub repository and needs an internet connection.</p>
